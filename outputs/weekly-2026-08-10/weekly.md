## Deep Product Question
When an AI agent truly starts acting for users, should the PM optimize for task automation, or prioritize user control and responsibility boundaries?

## Product Performance
- Break the agent experience into intent, planning, tool use, feedback, and recovery.
- Treat pause, undo, review, and escalation as first-class product capabilities.
- Track not only completion rate, but intervention rate, rollback rate, recovery time, and audit quality.

## Market Demand
- Enterprises are not buying automation alone; they are buying trustworthy scalable execution.
- Local and open-weight models improve privacy, latency, and cost, but make governance harder.
- Regulation and incidents will push high-risk industries to ask for auditable agents before broad deployment.

## Product Value
- User value: save time while preserving control over high-risk actions.
- Platform value: deeper workflow integration and feedback data.
- Business value: controllability, auditability, and responsibility boundaries become enterprise pricing and procurement advantages.
